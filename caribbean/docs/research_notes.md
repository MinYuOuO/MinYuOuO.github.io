# Caribbean Juice Bar (Johor) - Design & Branding Report

This report outlines the branding, visual design language, and content structure of the **Caribbean Juice Bar (Johor)** website to inform the development of our interactive food ordering and AR presentation prototype.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Vibe

The brand centers around **health, tropical freshness, and community**.

* **Vibe**: Clean, organic, inviting, and tropical.
* **Core Pillars**:
  * **Island Community**: A welcoming social hub.
  * **Sustainable Practices**: Eco-friendly packaging and support for local farmers.
  * **Pure Integrity**: Transparency in ingredients, 100% natural, no added sugar, and no fillers (like ice in smoothies).

---

## 2. Visual Design System

### A. Color Palette

The website uses a fresh, natural, and warm tropical palette:

* **Primary Backgrounds**: Clean white and soft cream/beige backgrounds to emphasize purity and freshness.
* **Accents**:
  * **Forest Green / Olive**: Represents nature, health, and organic ingredients.
  * **Warm Terra Cotta / Coral**: Evokes tropical warmth and sunset vibes.
  * **Soft Amber / Gold**: Represents sunshine and fresh fruits like mango and pineapple.
* **Typography Color**: Deep charcoal (`#080808` / `#151515`) for high readability.

### B. Typography

* **Headings**: Clean, elegant serif or high-contrast sans-serif fonts to feel premium yet organic.
* **Body Text**: Clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., Arial, Helvetica, or custom clean web fonts) to convey modern simplicity.

### C. Visual Elements

* **Imagery**: Large, vibrant photography of fresh fruits, colorful smoothie bowls, cold-pressed juices, and the cozy in-store boutique.
* **Iconography**: Clean, minimalist vector line art representing fruits, leaves, and sustainability symbols.

---

## 3. Menu & Offerings

The menu focuses on nutritious, fresh, and vegan-friendly options:

1. **Cold-Pressed Juices**: Nutrient-rich, made fresh daily with 40% more nutrients (e.g., Green Juices, Citrus Blends).
2. **Smoothies**: Thick, nutrient-dense smoothies made without ice or fillers.
3. **Smoothie Bowls**: Topped with fresh fruits, seeds, and granola (e.g., Acai, Mango, or Berry bases).
4. **Mocktails**: Refreshing tropical fruit mocktails.
5. **Vegan Snacks**: Health-focused vegan snacks and pastries.

---

## 4. Application to our AR Prototype

We will translate this design system into a premium web interface for the **FoodMe AR Prototype**:

* **Theme**: We will offer a **Light/Dark toggle**, with the default being a clean, premium light theme inspired by their website's pure, organic aesthetic (soft cream backgrounds, forest green accents, and warm coral highlights).
* **Card Design**: Minimalist cards with soft shadows, smooth hover transitions, and prominent "View in AR" buttons utilizing a warm coral-to-gold gradient.
* **AR Integration**: The AR viewer will let customers see 3D models of these colorful tropical items (like a detailed 3D Mango Smoothie Bowl or Cold-Pressed Juice bottle) sitting on their table.
