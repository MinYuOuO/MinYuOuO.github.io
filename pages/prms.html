Postgraduate Research Progress Monitoring System (PRMS)

The institutional single source of truth automating Graduate-on-Time (GoT) risk detection, ReBAC supervisory governance, and cryptographic audit locking.

Solo-developed across 8 months for Raffles University's Institute of Postgraduate Studies & Research (IPSRE). PRMS replaces vulnerable paper logbooks, fragmented email chains, and untracked verbal supervision meetings with an auditable multi-tier governance workflow built on Laravel 12, PHP 8.2+, MySQL 8, Docker, and cryptographic SHA-256 state sealing.

02 // Operational Impact

Before vs. After: Administrative Transformation

Resolving systemic vulnerabilities and manual overhead in university postgraduate research governance.

Legacy Manual Process (Before PRMS)

Fragmented Communication Channels

Supervision records were scattered across email threads, unversioned Excel spreadsheets, and informal messaging apps, resulting in lost milestone records.

Late Detection of Academic Delays

Struggling candidates and missed thesis milestones were only discovered at the end of maximum candidature tenure, causing severe Graduate-on-Time (GoT) failures.

Unverified Oral Agreements

Physical paper logbooks relied on verbal discussions with no verifiable record of action items, causing disputes when faculty supervisors transitioned.

Vulnerable to Retroactive Alteration

Physical progress report signatures could be backdated, lost, or misplaced with zero cryptographic tamper evidence or verifiable audit trails.

Manual Viva Clearance Bottlenecks

The Dean and faculty administrators had to manually verify paper publication reprints, ethics approvals, and meeting counts before scheduling viva defenses.

PRMS Single Source of Truth (After Implementation)

Centralized Institutional Platform

A single institutional web portal and relational database providing transparent, real-time visibility across all active Master and PhD candidatures.

Automated Nightly Risk Engine

Automated cron command scans all candidatures every 24 hours, evaluating tenure limits and meeting cadences to flag at-risk candidates automatically.

Digital Canvas Endorsements

Supervisors review concrete research targets and append on-screen vector signatures with precise microsecond timestamps and IP tracking.

Cryptographic SHA-256 State Seal

Evaluations are serialized and cryptographically hashed upon supervisor signature, locking the record against any subsequent tampering.

Automated Compliance Gate

Automated graduation clearance cross-validating Scopus/WoS publication indexation, ethics clearance, and rubric score thresholds before viva authorization.

03 Stage 03: Cryptographic State Seal

SHA-256 Serialization & Write-Once Lock

The system serializes the meeting record, vector points, and endorsement metadata into a normalized JSON payload. It computes a cryptographic SHA-256 digest, records the hash, and sets is_locked = true. Any retroactive tampering invalidates the hash and aborts access.
