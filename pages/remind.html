ReMind — Mobile Relationship Manager

Privacy-first, offline-first personal relationship manager helping users securely store friend details and contact logs on-device.

Designed for users seeking radical privacy and seamless personal interaction tracking. Built with Ionic, Angular, and Capacitor, ReMind persists 100% of user data locally inside an on-device SQLite database, guaranteeing complete data sovereignty and instantaneous interactions with zero cloud telemetry.
