// 02 · Hybrid Experience Architecture

Bridging Physical & Digital Heritage

Traces of Senai pairs tactile on-site community exploration across Kampung Baru Senai with a zero-install web companion—linking physical landmarks, elders, and print craft to conversational AI and WebAR.

Physical World · Kampung Baru Senai

On-Site Heritage Walk & Craft

  • Physical Trace Pack & Journal Collected at Stop 01 (Trace Station, paper sponsored by KPP-Antalis) so visitors can record their first impressions of Senai and collect stickers along the route.
  • 7 Real-World Landmarks & Physical QR Markers Participants walk through Kong Hay Tong, Tong Le Theatre, 1909 Senai Railway, Senai River, and Old Street, scanning physical QR markers (/scan/{qr_code_id}) at each stop.
  • Hands-On Hakka Culinary Session At Stop 07 (Hakka Flavors), local elders guide visitors step by step in making traditional Hakka Lei Cha (擂茶), Muah Chee (麻糍), and snacks.
  • The Physical Trace Wall Concludes the trail as a shared community installation where participants pin written reflections comparing how they saw Senai before and after the walk.
Digital Layer · Zero-Install Web App

Interactive AI Guide, Map & WebAR

  • Bilingual Onboarding & 4-Tier Audience Calibration EN/ZH language switch, frictionless Google/Facebook/OTP/Guest login, and 4 audience profiles (Primary, Secondary, University, Public/Elders) that tune AI depth and tone.
  • Hand-Illustrated Interactive Progress Map Custom panoramic map tracking locked vs. completed hotspots as visitors scan each stop, paired with pre-recorded bilingual stop narrations.
  • Trace Soul Voice-to-Voice AI (黄子松 / Wong Zi Song) Push-to-talk Qwen FunASR + DeepSeek SSE + Hakka-phrased CosyVoice WebSocket audio (389 ms TTFT) grounded in 139 curated oral-history records via MySQL ngram FULLTEXT RAG.
  • Kivicube WebAR Spatial Overlays (Stops 3–6) Browser-native fullscreen AR directly in Safari/Chrome reconstructing 1930s Tong Le Theatre, 1909 Senai Railway, Senai River life, and 1929–1939 Old Street.

// 05 · Systems Engineering & Infrastructure

Production Architecture & Technical Blueprints

Built on Laravel (PHP 8.3+/8.5) + Blade + Tailwind CSS v4 + Vite 8 + Turbo, MySQL 8.4, Redis, and hardened K3s + Cloudflare Zero Trust Tunnel. Switch tabs to inspect each subsystem schematic.

Challenge 01 // Retrieval & Cost

Zero-Vector-DB Bilingual RAG

Replaced a planned Node.js microservice with Native PHP + MySQL 8.4 ngram FULLTEXT weighted scoring (3.0×/2.5×/1.5×) plus LLM query expansion and strict historical guardrails—eliminating vector DB hosting while keeping LLM spend under $0.0015/user.

Challenge 02 // Mobile Audio Latency

389 ms WebSocket Streaming & iOS Unlock

Engineered a raw PHP TLS WebSocket client (TCP_NODELAY) piping CosyVoice audio chunks directly to browsers, paired with an interaction-driven iOS Safari autoplay unlock and a 3-tier failover service benchmarked against local 60 MB Piper VITS (wong_zi_song.onnx).

Challenge 03 // Security Hardening

Zero-Trust Hybrid K3s Migration

Hardened the deployment into a self-hosted K3s cluster keeping MySQL 8.4 private over WireGuard Flannel, routing all traffic via outbound-only Cloudflare Zero Trust Tunnels with non-root seccompProfile pods, HSTS, and Kivicube-safe CSP headers.
